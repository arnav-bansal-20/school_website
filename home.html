About School

D.A.V. Centenary Public School, Fatehabad, was established in March 1989, to meet the long-cherished demand of Fatehabad area under the aegis of D.A.V. College Managing Committee with a dream of turning it into an institution par excellence, which would be reckoned in the field of education.

D.A.V. History

Maharshi Dyanand Vedic Mission is a unit of Bihar Rajya Arya Pratinidhi Sabha. D.A.V. Movement founded in 1885 by Lala lajpat Raj has grown today as the largest Non-Government educational Organisation in the Country, managing over 700 educational institution in different parts of the country and abroad. D.A.V. institutions have rendered a Unique service.
