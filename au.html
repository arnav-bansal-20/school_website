ABOUT US

The greatest gift one can give to one's child is azest for living, a spirit of wonder and adventureand a confidence that the problems of life can be solved or endured.Our endeavour is to be an efficient institute committed towards innovation, knowledge, sharing and openness. We shall have passion for knowledge and excellence.D.A.V. Centenary Public School, Fatehabad, was established in March 1989, to meet the long-cherished demand of Fatehabad area under the aegis of D.A.V. College Managing Committee with a dream of turning it into an institution par excellence, which would be reckoned in the field of education. The citizens of Fatehabad and the parents at large co-operated in every way in this noble venture. With the efforts of well qualified and dedicated staff members and timely inspiration and guidance from the management the school today stands apart in excellence and has achieved a distinct position. During the last 25 years, this school has become synonymous with excellence, talent, commitment and wisdom. It focusses on developing a new cadre of students who will not only be creators but also visionaries; not only problem solvers but also thinkers.
